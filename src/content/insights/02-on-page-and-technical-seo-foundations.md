## 01. Semantic HTML Heading Hierarchy

One of the most common mistakes in visual web development is choosing heading tags (`H1`, `H2`, `H3`) based on font size rather than document structure. Search crawlers rely on heading hierarchy to understand the primary topic and subtopics of a page.

Every page should have one `H1` that names the core service or page subject, supported by logical `H2` and `H3` sections covering deliverables, process steps, and common questions.

## 02. Dedicated Pages Per Core Service

Listing ten distinct services on a single bulleted page makes it difficult for search engines or visitors to match a specific inquiry. Creating dedicated sections or pages with focused metadata for each core service helps visitors land directly on the information relevant to their need.

## 03. Technical Indexing & Performance Basics

Before launching any website, essential technical checks ensure search engines can crawl and index your pages cleanly:

- **XML Sitemap & Robots.txt:** Guide search crawlers to your canonical pages.
- **301 Redirect Mapping:** Preserve existing links when replacing an older website.
- **Asset Optimization:** Compress images and use clean layout containers so pages load smoothly on mobile connections.
