## 01. Why Unstructured Builds Become Difficult to Update

When a website is assembled hastily with inconsistent spacing and ad-hoc styles, even a small change—such as adding a new service section or updating a team bio—can break mobile layouts on other pages.

Investing in a clean component system and centralized design tokens from day one ensures that future pages can be added predictably.

## 02. Designing Editor-Friendly CMS Collections

Business owners should not have to worry about breaking the design grid when publishing an article or adding a project example. Structuring CMS fields with clear labels and consistent templates keeps the visual layout intact as your library of content grows.
