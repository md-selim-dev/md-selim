## 01. Avoiding the "Launch and Neglect" Cycle

Many small service businesses rebuild their website from scratch every few years because the site is neglected in between. Over time, services evolve, new questions arise, and old pages stop reflecting how the business actually operates.

A calm, consistent rhythm of updates—whether monthly or quarterly—keeps your positioning accurate and prevents the need for disruptive full rebuilds.

## 02. Turning Client Questions into Helpful Website Content

Some of the most practical website improvements come directly from conversations with prospective clients. Whenever prospects repeatedly ask the same question on discovery calls, adding a clear explanation or FAQ entry to your website helps future visitors find that answer immediately.
